Read this before you automate

Starter agents, real limits

Sources matter

An agent can only check a calendar, inbox, website, CRM, or analytics source when your AI setup actually gives it access. Every kit tells it to name missing sources instead of pretending.

Scheduling depends on your platform

Some AI plans support scheduled tasks and connected tools. Some do not. Run each agent manually until its output passes, then schedule only what your platform can really run.

Humans approve the sharp edges

Customer messages, prices, claims, bookings, quotes, posts, and commitments stay under human approval. The agent drafts and analyzes. You decide.
