What is the audit, exactly?

A highly detailed audit package that combines the criteria used by industry leaders into one detailed package. We ran it against five AIs, Grok, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Muse, and the full criteria are published at brobots.space/sao-audit.

What did the five AI platforms actually audit?

Each platform independently audited usaenergypartners.com from the same brief and the same score definition: how likely AI assistants are to find, understand, trust, and recommend the business when Texas business owners ask about commercial electricity costs. Scores: Grok 22, ChatGPT 54, Gemini 18, Claude 18 and Muse 17 out of 100.

Why is ChatGPT's score 54 when the others are 17 to 22?

ChatGPT found the same gaps as the other four. It weighted USAEP's clear positioning more heavily and treated the missing evidence as a trust ceiling rather than invisibility. The disagreement is reported openly, a five-platform audit is credible only if the outlier stays on the board.

What do we need from USA Energy Partners?

One short call with the owner, the PUCT broker registration number, and the real numbers behind the results, starting with the client who was overpaying by $10,000 a year. We bring the audits, the video, the design, and the build.

How is success measured after launch?

The same five-platform audit runs again 90 days after launch, under the same conditions (same brief, same score definition, same audit package), and the scores are published side by side with this report. The target: out of the Invisible band and into In the Running (50+), with AI's Pick (75+) as the stretch goal.
