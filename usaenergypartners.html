    Prove the numbers.

    The 95% / 99% / 93% / 100% metrics have no methodology, timeframe, or example anywhere. Every platform ranked this first or second.

  2. 02

  3. 03
    Give each service its own page.

    Four services live as tabs on a single page. One URL gives AI one thing to cite.

  4. 04
    Fix the freshness.

    Footer says Copyright 2019. The ERCOT history, the site's biggest block of content, ends in 2016. The company reads as dormant.

  5. 05
    Add the machine-readable layer.

    No business schema, no FAQ schema, empty meta description. The layer AI reads first is blank.

  6. 06
    Answer buyer questions in buyer language.

    Zero Q&A content site-wide. Buyers search "commercial electricity broker"; the site says "We Are Wisdom."

  7. 07
    Show the process, and how the broker gets paid.

    No platform could find what happens after contact, or the fee model. Competitors state it openly.

  8. 08
    Display the PUCT broker registration.

    The baseline qualification Texas buyers and AI check first, never stated on the site.

  9. 09
    Build third-party proof.

    Google Business Profile, named testimonials, reviews, social profiles. Today there is no corroboration anywhere.

  10. 10
