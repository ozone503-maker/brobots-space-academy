The Combined Audit Report

The BROBOTS engine crawled the site and ran 38 checks. The plumbing is fine, HTTPS clean, sitemap clean, no broken links. The house is empty: the page title is literally "USAEP Homepage," the first heading a crawler finds is "Contact Us," and there is no phone number anywhere on the site.

The consensus Top 10, merged from all five audits

  1. Prove the numbers. The 95% / 99% / 93% / 100% metrics have no methodology, timeframe, or example anywhere. Every platform ranked this first or second.
  2. Put people and contact details on the site. No named person, and no email or phone published as readable text, anywhere. One contact form exists, but AI crawlers can't see it. The video says "email Chris." The site never mentions him.
  3. Give each service its own page. Four services live as tabs on a single page. One URL gives AI one thing to cite.
  4. Fix the freshness. Footer says Copyright 2019. The ERCOT history, the site's biggest block of content, ends in 2016. The company reads as dormant.
  5. Add the machine-readable layer. No business schema, no FAQ schema, empty meta description. The layer AI reads first is blank.
  6. Answer buyer questions in buyer language. Zero Q&A content site-wide. Buyers search "commercial electricity broker"; the site says "We Are Wisdom."
  7. Show the process, and how the broker gets paid. No platform could find what happens after contact, or the fee model. Competitors state it openly.
  8. Display the PUCT broker registration. The baseline qualification Texas buyers and AI check first, never stated on the site.
  9. Build third-party proof. Google Business Profile, named testimonials, reviews, social profiles. Today there is no corroboration anywhere.
  10. Put the video and the free-review offer on the homepage. The company already owns its best sales asset. Its website doesn't have it.

Credibility notes: we checked the auditors too. The contact form does exist in a real browser, three platforms' crawlers couldn't see it, which makes the finding stronger, not weaker.
