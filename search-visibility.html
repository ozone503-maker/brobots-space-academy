The Muse AI visibility score
our own method

There is no industry standard for measuring AI visibility yet, so we built our own. Here is exactly how it works.

How we score

We ask a fixed set of real customer questions in each AI engine, in fresh sessions. Every answer can earn up to four points.

Signal in the answerPoints
Your brand is mentioned1
You are cited with a link to your site1
You appear first or in the top 31
The mention is positive1

Your score is the points earned divided by the points possible across every question and engine, shown out of 100.

Why it is fair

Same questions, same engines, fresh sessions, every month. That keeps the numbers comparable.

AI answers shift from one session to the next, so we read the trend over months, not any single result.

This is Brobots' own methodology for tracking progress. It is not an industry standard, and no one can promise a ranking or a mention.
