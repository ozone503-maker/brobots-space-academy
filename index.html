01

Direction

Before a pixel exists: what it looks like, what it sounds like, and why. Type, color, imagery, how things move, and the one thing people should remember. Decided up front, on purpose, in writing.

02

The map

Every section gets a job. Nav, opening, the story, the proof, the ask, the form, the footer — and how each one hands you to the next. Phone first, because that's where they'll actually see it.

03

The one image

One picture or idea does the heavy lifting. How it's framed, how it enters, what happens when you scroll past it. If a visitor remembers one thing about your site, this is the thing.

04

Motion

How the page arrives. What moves as you scroll. Hovers, transitions, the small stuff. Every movement has to earn its place — anything there just to look busy gets cut.

05

Build

Actual construction, in stages. Components, responsive behavior, forms, SEO, accessibility, speed. Each stage tested before the next starts. Nothing that already worked gets broken along the way.

06

The de-slop pass

A human goes through the whole thing hunting for anything generic. Default fonts. Stock-photo energy. Spacing that's technically correct and completely dead. Copy that could belong to any company on earth. It all gets ripped out and replaced with something that could only be yours.

07

Launch audit

Real devices, real browsers, real load times. Forms that actually send. Links that actually go. Everything ranked by what it costs you if it stays broken, then fixed. Then it ships.
