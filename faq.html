QUESTIONS,
ANSWERED STRAIGHT.

25 answers · the same ones we give the AI · brobots.space

1. What is BROBOTS?

BROBOTS is the Space Factory in Mountain View, Hawaiʻi — a creative studio that builds websites, online stores, film, and trained AI bots. Its line is simple: an agency that uses AI, not one that is one. Every build is finished by a human pass that removes anything that reads as machine-made.

2. What does BROBOTS build?

Websites, stores, original film and video, and custom BroBots — trained AI specialists that sound like you and know your business. It also runs Creator Rooms (browser tools like the Paste-Up Desk and Audio Lab) and Biorama, its social media homesteading product.

3. How much does a website cost at BROBOTS?

Website builds come in three locked tiers: Tier 1 is $495, Tier 2 is $2,495, and Tier 3 is $4,995. A 50% deposit starts the build, and financing is available. À la carte add-ons — extra pages, a chatbot, video, an SAO pack — can be added to any tier.

4. What is included in Tier 1 ($495)?

Tier 1 is a custom static site — no bots, no moving assets — with SEO included, an SAO pack (30 Q&As plus FAQ schema), a blog with 3 launch articles, and DIY agent access. BROBOTS describes it as everything a small business needs to look real and get found.

5. What is included in Tier 2 ($2,495)?

Tier 2 includes everything in Tier 1, plus an AI chatbot, a 60-second promo video, and a motion graphics pack. It is the tier where, in BROBOTS' words, your site talks back and moves.

6. What is included in Tier 3 ($4,995)?

Tier 3 includes everything in Tier 2, plus a custom AI agent built for one of your real workflows, with a human approval step. On Shopify, that agent doesn't just chat — it walks customers from first question to completed checkout.

7. What is a BroBot?

A BroBot is a hand-trained AI specialist that knows your business, sounds like you, and works the counter while you sleep. BROBOTS' example is Ipo, who answers ingredient and skin questions for Fruity Puppy. In their words: you're not buying a bot, you're recruiting a graduate.

8. How much does a custom BroBot cost?

There are three BroBot packages: Wingman at $497 (one primary function, custom tone, email or website chat), Sidekick at $1,497 (full personality and voice, up to 100 Q&As, website embedding, custom avatar), and Legend at $4,997 (multi-function logic, trained on your materials, lifetime update support). Each starts with a 50% deposit.

9. What is the difference between the AI chatbot add-on and a custom AI agent?

The AI chatbot ($650 à la carte, included in Tier 2) answers visitors on your site. A custom AI agent ($3,000 à la carte, included in Tier 3) is built for one of your real workflows — for example, taking a Shopify customer from first question to completed checkout — and includes a human approval step.

10. What is SAO?

SAO is search answer optimization. Instead of only chasing search rankings, your pages carry straight answers to the questions your customers already ask AI — so your business can be found on Google and Bing and quoted in AI answers from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Grok.

11. What is in the SAO pack?

The SAO pack is 30 Q&As written for your business, plus FAQ schema (the structured data that helps search engines and AI read those answers). It's included in every website tier, or $500 on its own.

12. Can BROBOTS guarantee I will rank first or get recommended by AI?

No — and they say so on the site: no one can promise a ranking. What BROBOTS does is make you easy to find and easy to quote: clean structure, real page titles and descriptions, structured data, fast pages, and straight answers.

13. What is the free SAO audit?

The free SAO audit crawls your website, then asks the 30 questions your customers ask AI and checks whether your site actually answers them — 38 checks in all. It takes about 2–4 minutes, and BROBOTS says it never shares your results.

14. Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. Your code, your domain, your accounts — if you leave, it leaves with you. BROBOTS is explicit about this: no hostage hosting, and no rented site you lose when you stop paying.

15. What is the DIY agent?

Every website tier includes the DIY agent, so you can make your own site changes whenever you want instead of waiting on a developer. DIY agent access is also available on its own for $99/mo.

16. How does payment work?

Every website tier and BroBot package starts with a 50% deposit — that's what starts the build. Financing is available on website builds. Add-ons and à la carte items are priced individually on the services page.

17. How long does a website build take?

BROBOTS doesn't publish fixed timelines, because every build runs the same seven passes — direction, the map, the one image, motion, build, the de-slop pass, and a launch audit — and the scope sets the schedule. Send the brief through the contact form and you'll get a straight answer for your project.

18. Who is BROBOTS for?

Small businesses that need to look real and get found: a business with no presence at all, one whose website has been sitting there for years, or one with traffic and no time. The giveaway page puts it plainly — if you're building something real, they want to build with you.

19. Does BROBOTS shoot video too?

Yes — original footage, editing, score, and cutdowns for every platform, shot and cut in-house. A 60-second promo video is included in Tier 2, or $1,500 à la carte; a motion graphics pack is $800.

20. What are the BROBOTS monthly giveaways?

Every month BROBOTS gives away three things, no purchase necessary: a free Tier 1 website (with a free .space domain and a year of hosting), a free AI search makeover for an old website, and a free custom chatbot trained on your company. Details and the application are on the FREE_TECH page.

21. How do the giveaways work — who wins?

You apply on the FREE_TECH page (it takes about two minutes), one entry per business per month. Winners aren't drawn at random — each giveaway goes to the business that's the best fit, the one BROBOTS can do the most for. Everybody else gets a follow-up offer; in their words, nobody leaves empty-handed.

22. Can I add extras to a website tier?

Yes — à la carte, only if you want it: extra page $150, blog or news article $200, SAO pack $500, AI chatbot $650, motion graphics pack $800, 60-second promo video $1,500, custom AI agent (one workflow) $3,000, and DIY agent access $99/mo.

23. What is Biorama?

Biorama is BROBOTS' social media homesteading product: instead of renting a profile, you claim a bowl — your own domain, repo, and hosting, with an AI caretaker keeping it running. It costs $99 once, with no rent after that.

24. Can my team or agency use BROBOTS tools?

The Creator Rooms tools — Paste-Up Desk, Audio Lab, Video Bench, Render Bay, and Fun Locker — are available for team installs, not just individual access. Send your setup details through the contact form and BROBOTS will price it for your team.

25. How do I contact BROBOTS or start a project?

Use the brief form on brobots.space — name, email, what you're building, and a budget band — or email brobots.space@gmail.com. Their instruction: send the messy version; figuring out the shape is part of the job.
