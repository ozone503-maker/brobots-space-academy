## What SAO is
SAO = Search Answer Optimization. When a customer asks an AI "who's the best roofer near me," the AI answers from websites it can read and quote. SAO means your website answers the exact questions customers ask AI — so the AI recommends you instead of your competitor.

## What the free audit does
1. Crawls the visitor's website (takes 2–4 minutes).
2. Runs 38 checks across 6 categories:
   - Clarity — is it obvious what you do and where?
   - Authority — licenses, credentials, proof, reviews
   - Relevance — do your services match what customers search?
   - Consistency — name, phone, hours match everywhere
   - AI Readability — can AI systems parse and quote your pages?
   - Question Coverage — the big one (see below)
3. Asks the 30 questions customers ask AI about their business type (cost, trust, process, problems, local availability) and checks whether the site answers each one: Answered, Partial, or Missing.
4. Scores 0–100 with a grade band:
   - Invisible — AI can't see you
   - Overlooked — seen but not picked
   - In the Running — competitive
   - AI's Pick — the one AI recommends
5. If the basics are missing (no clear service or location), the score is capped at 49 no matter what.
6. Gives the 5 highest-impact fixes first, ranked by points recovered.

Key line you may use: "Every missing answer is a page your competitors get to write first."
