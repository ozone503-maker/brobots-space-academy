HOW IT WORKS
Left half of the range = DRIVE. Hold your left thumb dragged away (◀) to enter GUARD — releasing a guard can never fire an attack, by design.
Right half = WEAVE. That thumb attacks; the mode above decides how it is read.
CLENCH (the special): put both thumbs down and squeeze them toward each other.
The green strip is the intent indicator — it always shows what the game thinks you're doing, live. No score is shown during a session. Results land at the end.
