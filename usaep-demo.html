Questions Texas businesses ask

What does a commercial energy broker do?

Because the Texas electricity market is deregulated, many retail energy providers compete for business customers. We identify the best provider for the profile of your energy load, then review the contract type and language so a good rate is not undone by the fine print.

What is power factor correction?

Power is often not used efficiently, and the utility can penalize the user for it. We identify and rectify the cause, stopping unnecessary penalties.

What is a tax-exempt study?

A sales-and-use tax study identifies whether your business qualifies for tax-exempt status, and can help recover a portion of incorrectly paid funds.

What happens after I sign a contract?

We stay your advocate and single point of contact. If there is an issue, it is our responsibility to address it and get it fixed on your behalf.
