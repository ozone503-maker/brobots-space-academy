## Lesson intro
There are dozens of ways to make money with AI. Most lists are affiliate bait — somebody getting paid to recommend the tool. This one is different: every model below is filtered through what actually works for a shop like yours. Four are crossed out. They'll cost you more than they earn you.

## Part 1 — Resell (sell under your brand, others do fulfillment)
1. Resell AI SEO/AEO — you own the client relationship.
2. Branded CRM — one niche, AI follow-up built in.
3. Resell AI chatbots — lead qualification and customer support.
4. Resell AI receptionists — answer inquiries, book appointments.

## Part 2 — Install & maintain (charge for setup, testing, upkeep)
5. AI phone receptionist for local businesses.
6. Internal assistant built on the company's own documents.
7. Meeting notes wired to CRM updates and follow-up drafts.
8. Lead-routing workflows that qualify and alert.
9. Invoice workflows with human approval before money moves.

## Part 3 — ❌ Crossed out: content services
Podcast clipping, demo videos, ghostwriting, ad creative. We don't create content or promise views. Know your lane.

## Part 4 — Digital products (build once, sell many)
10. Niche workflow templates with setup instructions.
11. Industry-specific prompt packs with tested examples.
12. Calculators and interactive tools for a narrow audience.
13. Courses with an AI study assistant.
14. Workbooks, checklists, implementation kits.

## Part 5 — Consulting & training (high-margin human time)
15. AI workflow audits — find the repetitive work, prioritize what to automate.
16. Role-specific workshops for sales, marketing, ops.
17. AI tool selection — kill overlapping subscriptions.
18. Agent QA — test existing AI agents for errors and missed handoffs.
19. Monthly optimization retainers.

## The takeaway
Start with the audit (free, automated). Sell the fix (productized). Keep the relationship (retainer). That's the whole business.
