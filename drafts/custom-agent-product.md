# DRAFT — Custom AI Agent product spec (Tier 3 / à la carte $3,000)

## What it is
One workflow, handled by a small team of AI agents that never clock out.
Example workflows: lead follow-up, quote drafting, review responses, inbox triage, appointment reminders.

## How it's built
1. **Shared business brief** — one source document: your services, prices, tone, what the agents may never claim, and what a qualified lead looks like. Every agent reads this before working.
2. **Specialized agents, one job each** — e.g. researcher, drafter, checker. No single agent doing everything.
3. **Explicit handoffs** — each agent passes a defined output to the next. No vibes, no guessing.
4. **Human approval step** — nothing customer-facing goes out without your eyes until you say otherwise.

## What's included
- The business brief, written with you
- The agents, wired and tested on your real workflow
- The approval step (you stay in control)
- 30 days of tuning after launch

## What it doesn't do
- Doesn't replace you. It does the repetitive parts; you make the calls.
- We don't build apps and we don't promise social media views. This is a workflow platform, not magic.

## Price
$3,000 one workflow. Included in Tier 3 ($4,995).
